What the results mean

Medium fix means the Cardmarket expansion name, the card name, and the full collector number (letter suffixes included) match one English catalogue card. It does not use the short code in parentheses, so MEW 025 can match set 151. A Japanese expansion is not matched to an English set that happens to share a code.

385 existing links agree visually. Another 72 existing links are wrong-language matches: their Japanese and English cards share artwork, but are different prints. 165 proposed links comprise 96 image-checked proposals, one image-checked proposal requiring a collector-number correction, and 68 metadata-only proposals. Coverage and remaining work

The report includes all 3,400 products with a stored listing-image URL. It does not claim that every photo has been manually inspected. “Unresolved” rows retain metadata triage only. Cards without an official image cannot receive a two-image verification. Cached thumbnails do not reliably resolve every foil, edition or finish detail.

English, Japanese, Traditional Chinese and Simplified Chinese catalogue records were available for candidate checks. Indonesian/Thai and missing Chinese/Japanese sets need the correct catalogue print before an exact link is possible. A cross-language artwork relationship never authorizes an exact-print link.

The 30th candidates were identified by their visible anniversary stamp and name; TCGdex’s 30th Classic Collection records use sequential IDs and lack official art. The two Darkrai & Cresselia halves are fixed: 99 is catalogue 019 and 100 is catalogue 020. The original Gists have not been republished.
